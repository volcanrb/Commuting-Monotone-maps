## Math_XMUM Brouwer formalization

The files `BrouwerScarf.lean` and `BrouwerStandardSimplex.lean` contain
adapted source from the camera-ready artifact of:

- Yuwei Lyu and Kai Li, *Formalizing Scarf, Brouwer, and Nash in Lean*;
- repository: <https://github.com/Solo-ary/Game-Theory-Formalization>;
- tag: `camera-ready-icml2026`;
- commit: `8e252837d9322609c5d7de98c2e6948dd5390ade`.

`BrouwerStandardSimplex.lean` additionally incorporates only Lean-version
compatibility changes from the maintained MIT-licensed upstream repository
<https://github.com/math-xmum/Brouwer>, commit
`c02205edf347ad45f0d62db85497598ba2c4291e`. Its mathematical statements and
Scarf-to-Brouwer argument remain those of the camera-ready artifact.
